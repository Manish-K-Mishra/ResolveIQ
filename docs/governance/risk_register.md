# Risk register (Governance Framework §2)

Scale: likelihood and impact are **Low / Medium / High**. They are judged from the measured
evidence linked in each row; nothing here is a hoped-for property of a model. Owners are role
names. Each owner's acceptance of the residual risk is **to be confirmed by the project owner**.

Evidence runs: `evaluation/results/final_offline_default` (validation 80, kill switch on),
`final_offline_autosend`, `final_llm_ollama_gemma4_generated`,
`final_llm_anthropic_haiku45_generated`, and `final_offline_development` (500, **in-sample** for
the offline classifier). Tables: [final_report_tables.md](../../evaluation/results/final_report_tables.md).

Tests: `test_private_data_in_outbound_text_is_blocked` (test_grounding.py), `test_private_data_in_the_answer_is_blocked` (test_generation.py), `test_ticket_pii_is_masked_only_for_hosted_providers`, `test_cache_holds_no_secrets_and_no_ticket_text` (test_reliability.py). | Low to medium. | R-03 | A customer's input is treated as an instruction | Medium | High | `contains_injection` screens the ticket after normalisation (NFKC, zero-width characters removed, digit-for-letter swaps undone). A match **blocks** the ticket and stores the masked input for review. Model output is constrained by schemas: PR-01 accepts only the 22 intents, and PR-02 forbids extra fields. A generated answer that cites a passage outside the approved set is blocked. Tests: `test_injection_variants_escalate`, `test_injection_variants_are_detected_n8`, `test_injection_screen_has_no_false_positives_on_supplied_data` (test_routing.py), `test_engineered_injection_ticket_is_blocked_end_to_end_via_cli` (test_ai_graph.py), `test_citation_outside_retrieved_set_is_blocked` (test_grounding.py). | Medium. Detection is regex-only (P-03): a paraphrased injection can pass. The routing decision itself is deterministic Python and cannot be changed by ticket text. An extractive answer can only be a verbatim approved passage. | Engineering lead | [architecture.md §5](../architecture.md) |
| R-04 | Some customer groups receive worse answers | High (measured) | Medium | Tier, region, channel and fluency are **not** routing inputs (`test_enterprise_tier_is_not_escalated_by_tier_alone`, test_routing.py). A reproducible segment audit (`scripts/fairness_audit.py`) feeds the report tables. | High. The under-5-point target is **not met**. The largest resolution gap is ticket length: 36.5 points offline (58.1% short against 21.6% long), caused by the lexical score diluting on long tickets. Region quality has a 37.7-point spread on groups of 7 to 27 tickets. The gaps show as more escalation, not as worse answers sent. | Project owner | [fairness_audit.md](fairness_audit.md) |
| R-05 | The documentation the system relies on goes stale | Medium | High | Only documents in the policy's `intent_documents` approval map can support an answer (`route.py`; `test_evidence_must_belong_to_the_predicted_intent`, `test_real_passage_that_was_not_approved_is_blocked`). A stale approved article would be quoted faithfully. | Documentation owner (technical writer) | `data/documentation.json`; [incident_response.md](incident_response.md) |
| R-06 | The model provider becomes unavailable | Medium | Medium | Per-request timeout, bounded retries and a wall-clock deadline. A circuit breaker opens after 3 consecutive failures and half-opens after a cooldown. A failed call escalates the ticket and never crashes the batch. The offline classifier needs no provider. Tests: `test_slow_streaming_provider_hits_wall_clock_deadline`, `test_circuit_breaker_opens_after_consecutive_failures_and_stops_calling` (test_model_provider.py), `test_persistent_429_escalates_cleanly_with_bounded_retries` (test_reliability.py), `test_half_open_breaker_admits_exactly_one_trial_call_across_threads` (test_api.py). | Low. | R-07 | Latency degrades under load | Medium | Medium | Offline p95 is 0.0012 s. `/metrics` exposes request and latency series to Prometheus/Grafana (`api.py`, `metrics.py`; `test_metrics_expose_counters_that_increment`, test_api.py). The deadline bounds the slowest call. | Medium. LLM modes miss the < 3 s p95 target: 5.7263 s with Ollama gemma4:e2b and 5.1164 s with Claude Haiku 4.5, measured sequentially on one machine. **No load test has been run**, so behaviour under concurrent load is unmeasured. | Engineering lead | [final_report_tables.md](../../evaluation/results/final_report_tables.md) (Latency appendix) |

Tests: `test_tokens_and_cost_are_recorded_per_decision`, `test_usage_metrics_project_monthly_cost_with_a_stated_volume` (test_reliability.py). | Low. ## Project-specific risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation in the design (enforced by) | Residual risk | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 | Validation overstates generalisation: 62 of 80 validation bodies are exact copies of development bodies | Certain (measured) | Medium | Thresholds come from grouped out-of-fold development data (`scripts/derive_routing_policy.py`; `test_routing_policy_reproduces_from_development_data`, test_env_setup.py). Every metrics report computes and states the overlap (`test_training_overlap_caveat_is_computed_for_the_actual_input`, test_evaluation.py). The overlap affects **the offline classifier and the development-derived thresholds only**. The LLM classifiers were not trained on development data. | Medium. Offline intent accuracy of 0.975 is inflated. The hidden 120-ticket set was never available, so no held-out figure exists. | Project owner | [architecture.md §8](../architecture.md) |
| P-02 | Labels are inconsistent: `expected_route` differs for identical bodies in 35 of 78 duplicate groups | Certain (measured) | Medium | Routing is driven by intent, safety rules and evidence, not by imitating the label. Precision and violations are reported alongside accuracy. | Medium. Routing accuracy and auto precision have a ceiling below 100%, and part of every "wrong" figure is label noise. | Support operations lead | [architecture.md §8](../architecture.md) |
| P-03 | Injection detection is pattern-based only | Medium | High | Same as R-03, plus paraphrased-redirection patterns added after the independent review (`test_paraphrased_redirection_is_flagged`, `test_ordinary_support_language_is_not_flagged`, `test_no_labelled_ticket_is_flagged_as_injection`, test_review_fixes.py). | Medium. A novel paraphrase can still pass undetected. | Engineering lead | [architecture.md §10](../architecture.md) |
| P-04 | The grounding proxy over-blocks faithful generated answers | High (measured) | Low (fail-safe) | `support_check` requires 0.6 content-word overlap with the cited passages (`guardrails.py` `SUPPORT_MIN_OVERLAP`; `test_faithful_paraphrases_clear_the_overlap_threshold`, `test_claims_that_borrow_few_passage_words_fall_below_the_threshold`, test_generation.py). | Medium. Claude Haiku 4.5 had 10 grounding blocks. On PE review, most are faithful paraphrases that fail the 0.6 overlap: false positives that go to a human. About 3 contain claims about the customer's own situation that the documentation does not support (VAL-0005, VAL-0057, VAL-0071), and those were correctly blocked. **Decision: the threshold was not retuned on validation**, because tuning it on the gate would overfit. The cost is less automation (Claude resolution 23.8% if released). | Engineering lead | [final_llm_anthropic_haiku45_generated/decisions.json](../../evaluation/results/final_llm_anthropic_haiku45_generated/decisions.json) |
| P-05 | Confidence is not calibrated | Certain (measured) | Medium | The confidence floor applies to the classifier's own score, and a missing score counts as 0. | Medium. The largest calibration gap is 59.8 points on validation offline and 21.9 points on development, against a ±5-point target. LLM-stated confidence is not calibrated at all (55.0 with Claude, 60.0 with Ollama). The 0.70 floor was validated for the offline classifier only. | Engineering lead | [final_report_tables.md](../../evaluation/results/final_report_tables.md) (Calibration appendix) |
| P-06 | Human quality measures are missing | Certain | Medium | `human_review_sample.csv` is written by every run for a two-assessor review. | High until done. The hallucination rate and the satisfaction proxy are **not measured**. | Support operations lead | [final_report_tables.md](../../evaluation/results/final_report_tables.md) |

Prose can still drift from the code between slices. | Engineering lead | [README.md](../../README.md) |
